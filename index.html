Make room
for harmony.

Six strings. One clear gauge. A native guitar tuner that helps you find your pitch and get back to playing.

Release updates

The first Mac download is being prepared.

01

Follow the sound.

Live frequency and a bright cents gauge show flat, sharp, and the green sweet spot in between.

02

Find your tuning.

Standard, Drop D, Half step down, Open G, and DADGAD. Let Okarina find the string, or select one yourself.

03

Keep it local.

Your microphone audio is analyzed on your device. Nothing is saved or sent to a server.

READY WHEN YOU ARE

Pluck. Adjust. Play.

Open the app, choose Start tuning, and allow microphone access. Pluck one open string and mute the others. Tighten gently if it’s flat; loosen if it’s sharp. Green means you’re within 5 cents of the target.

Experimental software. A₄ reference is adjustable from 430–450 Hz. iPhone and Apple Watch versions are in development; the public download is for Mac.
