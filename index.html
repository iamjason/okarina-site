Released September 13, 2026 · Release notes

01

Follow the sound.

Live frequency and a bright cents gauge show flat, sharp, and the green sweet spot in between.

02

Find your tuning.

Five guitar tunings: Standard, Drop D, Half step down, Open G, and DADGAD. Three ukulele tunings: High G, Low G, and Baritone. Let Okarina find the string, or select one yourself.

03

Keep it local.

Your microphone audio is analyzed on your device. Nothing is saved or sent to a server.

READY WHEN YOU ARE

Pluck. Adjust. Play.

Open the app, choose a tuning for your instrument, select Start tuning, and allow microphone access. Pluck one open string and mute the others. Tighten gently if it’s flat; loosen if it’s sharp. Green means you’re within 5 cents of the target.

Experimental software. A₄ reference is adjustable from 430–450 Hz. iPhone and Apple Watch versions are in development; the public download is for Mac.
